About me

Background

I am a Computer Science master's student at Oregon State University, graduating in December 2027. I came to computer science through human-computer interaction, which I focused on as an undergraduate, and I chose to continue at the graduate level to go deeper into it. Taking HCI coursework at the graduate level also puts me in a position to work with faculty and other graduate students on research, which is part of why I am here.

That background shapes how I approach problems and design solutions, always considering the user's perspective and needs. I am passionate about creating software that is not only functional but also provides a great user experience. By starting from the user's perspective, I can view it from a more holistic standpoint.

How this shapes my goals

Alongside my coursework in grad school, I work at OSU IT Service Desk, where I support the entire campus community with technical issues. The job has taught me more about how users interact with technology and softwares than any class could, largely because I get to observe where it fails rather than where it works.

The same interest in user-centered design drives my approach through my Pencil2D contributions. Before apply any fixes, I conduct a usability study for the two problems it surfaced.

I am still narrowing down exactly what kind of role I am interested in. I am drawn to positions that allow me to work where software meets the people using it, whether that is frontend development, UX design, or the support side. Meanwhile, I'm working on developing my skills with personal frontend projects.
